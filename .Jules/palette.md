## 2025-05-14 - [CommandBar Keyboard Navigation & ARIA]
**Learning:** For terminal-style workstation applications, a "fingers on keys" experience is a primary UX requirement. Users expect to navigate suggestions and execute commands without switching to the mouse. Implementing the ARIA combobox pattern (`role="combobox"`, `aria-activedescendant`) ensures this efficiency is accessible to screen reader users.
**Action:** Always prioritize keyboard shortcuts and ARIA-compliant autocomplete patterns for primary navigation/command inputs.
