## 2025-05-15 - Bloomberg Terminal CommandBar Accessibility
**Learning:** Institutional terminal users rely heavily on keyboard shortcuts and rapid command entry. Standard autocomplete patterns (ARIA combobox) are essential for accessibility and speed. Resetting the selection on every input change prevents accidental submission of the previous highlight.
**Action:** Always implement full keyboard navigation (Arrow keys, Enter, Escape) and ARIA attributes for command-driven interfaces.
